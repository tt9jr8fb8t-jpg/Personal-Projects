The Story

Built for people who love to read.

Gnos started as a personal project — a frustration with reading apps that either lived entirely in the cloud, required subscriptions, or cluttered the experience with social features nobody asked for.

Reading is private. It's intimate. Your library deserves to stay on your machine, in a format you own, with an interface that gets out of the way.

Gnos is a desktop application that brings your books, audiobooks, notes, and study tools into one beautiful space. No account needed. No data leaves your computer. No in-app purchases.

Principles

What Gnos believes in

Every product decision flows from a handful of beliefs about what software should feel like.

01

Local-first, always

Your books, notes, and reading history belong to you. Gnos stores everything on your machine using a file structure you can browse, back up, and own. No cloud sync means no dependency on any server — Gnos works offline forever.

02

Your content, your rules

Gnos is a distraction-free environment built around your library. Upload your own books, audiobooks, and notes — there is no store, no recommended content, and no in-app purchases. What you bring in is all that's here. No upsells. No lock-in.

03

Beautiful defaults

Gnos ships with six hand-crafted themes — including the signature warm sepia — so it looks great out of the box without any configuration. Great typography and spacing are non-negotiable, not optional extras.

04

Study and reading, together

The best learning happens at the intersection of reading and writing. Gnos keeps your Markdown notebooks, flashcards, and sketchbook right next to your books so you can learn actively without switching between a dozen different apps.

Under the Hood

Built on solid foundations

Gnos is a Tauri + React application — fast, lightweight, and genuinely native on every platform.

⚡

Tauri

A Rust-powered desktop framework that produces tiny, fast, native app bundles — much smaller than Electron alternatives.

⚛️

React 19

The UI is built with the latest React, with Zustand for state management and Vite for a fast development experience.

📐

Excalidraw + KaTeX

The sketchbook canvas uses Excalidraw's infinite whiteboard engine. Math notes render beautifully with KaTeX.

Future

Future

Gnos will eventually offer opt-in cloud features — sync across devices, encrypted backup, and seamless handoff between desktop and mobile. Local-first will always remain the default; cloud will always be a choice, never a requirement.
